Maintenance FAQs

Support & Maintenance Questions

Everything you need to know about our ongoing maintenance services.

Can you maintain a website that wasn’t originally built by TechNest Studio?

Yes. We conduct a preliminary code and security audit to evaluate the existing architecture, identify vulnerabilities or performance bottlenecks, and establish clean maintenance workflows for your existing website or application.

What is the turnaround time for bug fixes or content updates?

Critical issues (such as broken checkout or submission forms) are prioritized with immediate 24-hour response times. Routine content adjustments or minor feature additions are typically deployed within 24 to 48 business hours.

Do you require long-term lock-in contracts for maintenance?

No. We offer flexible month-to-month maintenance agreements as well as annual retainers. You can pause or cancel support at any time with 30 days’ notice.
